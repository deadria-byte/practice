# AKR Operations — Restaurant Skill Lab
### Path to $3,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue by December 31, 2026

Prepared July 26, 2026. Starting point: zero paying subscribers, product not yet launched.

## The Math
- Timeline: 22 to 23 weeks from today to December 31, 2026.
- Pricing: $97 Basic (downgrade path only, kept off the homepage), $297 Pro (default homepage offer), $597 Enterprise.
- At Pro tier pricing alone, 11 paying subscribers equals $3,267 in monthly recurring revenue, clearing the $3,000 target with room to spare.
- This plan targets 10 to 12 paying subscribers by December 31, weighted toward Pro tier, with any Basic or Enterprise mix treated as upside or buffer.
- Owner time constraint is fixed at under 2 hours per week. Every action below is designed to fit inside that ceiling: review, approve, and direct, not build by hand.

## Phase Overview

| Phase | Weeks | Dates | Focus | Paying Subscribers |

## Week by Week Action Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch Close-Out (Weeks 1 to 3, Jul 27 to Aug 16)
Every P1 blocker closed before a single dollar of paid traffic is spent. Nothing in this phase is optional.

**Week 1 (Jul 27 to Aug 2)**
- Finalize and execute Terms of Service with the compliance liability carve out.
- Add the food safety training disclaimer to all compliance content templates.
- Start Resend domain warm up and configure DKIM, DMARC, and SPF.
- Draft the Privacy Policy covering the quiz, email capture, and Stripe PCI scope.

**Week 2 (Aug 3 to Aug 9)**
- Configure CAN SPAM compliance inside Resend.
- Add auto renewal disclosure to the Stripe checkout flow.
- Build the cancellation save sequence in Make.com.
- Build the Supabase 14 day re engagement trigger.

**Week 3 (Aug 10 to Aug 16)**
- Publish the live landing page. Basic tier stays off the homepage, Pro tier is the default offer.
- Set up Make.com error alerts and a manual fallback checklist.
- Recruit and confirm 3 free trial users from DeAdria's F&B network for testimonials.
- Checkpoint: confirm all P1 legal and operational items are closed before Phase 2 begins.

### Phase 2: Beta and Content Build (Weeks 4 to 7, Aug 17 to Sep 13)
Prove the product works on real trial users and build the content library that inbound traffic will need.

**Week 4 (Aug 17 to Aug 23)**
- Onboard the 3 free trial users into Kajabi.
- Launch the Restaurant Profit Leak Audit quiz as the lead magnet.
- Publish the 5 email nurture sequence tied to the quiz.

**Week 5 (Aug 24 to Aug 30)**
- Run the Claude script to Cowork asset extraction pipeline for the Labor and Staffing pillar. Load into Kajabi.
- Check in with trial users, log friction points.

**Week 6 (Aug 31 to Sep 6)**
- Produce Food Cost and Inventory pillar content.
- Request written testimonials or short video testimonials from trial users.

**Week 7 (Sep 7 to Sep 13)**
- Produce Customer Service and Recovery pillar content.
- Checkpoint: at least one trial user converted or committed to convert to a paid Pro tier at launch.

### Phase 3: Public Launch and Inbound (Weeks 8 to 11, Sep 14 to Oct 11)
Go live. Let the lead magnet and SEO content do the acquisition work while the owner stays under the 2 hour weekly cap.

**Week 8 (Sep 14 to Sep 20)**
- Public launch of restaurantskilllab.com under the AKR Operations brand.
- Announce to DeAdria's F&B network and relevant LinkedIn audience.
- Turn on Tidio chat support widget.

**Week 9 (Sep 21 to Sep 27)**
- Publish Daily Operations and Systems pillar content.
- Monitor quiz to email to trial conversion rate weekly.

**Week 10 (Sep 28 to Oct 4)**
- Publish Management and Leadership pillar content.
- Target: first 1 to 2 paid Pro tier subscribers.

**Week 11 (Oct 5 to Oct 11)**
- Publish AI Business Operations pillar content, the core differentiator.
- Checkpoint: 3 cumulative paying subscribers, all six content pillars live in Kajabi.

### Phase 4: Outbound Activation (Weeks 12 to 16, Oct 12 to Nov 15)
Layer cold outreach on top of inbound now that the list, content, and testimonials exist to support it.

**Week 12 (Oct 12 to Oct 18)**
- Build the independent and small to medium restaurant owner prospect list in Apollo.io.
- Draft cold outreach sequences in Instantly or Smartlead.

**Week 13 (Oct 19 to Oct 25)**
- Launch cold outreach campaign.
- Monitor reply rate and adjust messaging weekly.

**Week 14 (Oct 26 to Nov 1)**
- Introduce prompt templates by restaurant category: QSR, casual dining, bar, cafe.
- Target: 5 cumulative paying subscribers.

**Week 15 (Nov 2 to Nov 8)**
- Review Delivery Agent output quality. Add Claude API quality scoring and regeneration logic.
- Target: 6 to 7 cumulative paying subscribers.

**Week 16 (Nov 9 to Nov 15)**
- Checkpoint: 8 cumulative paying subscribers. If behind, increase outreach volume before adding new tactics.

### Phase 5: Final Push (Weeks 17 to 23, Nov 16 to Dec 31)
Close the remaining gap to $3,000 MRR using referral, retention, and a bounded holiday promotion. No new channels this late.

**Week 17 (Nov 16 to Nov 22)**
- Activate the Upsell Agent groundwork ahead of the 10 client trigger.
- Ask current subscribers for referrals with a defined incentive.

**Week 18 (Nov 23 to Nov 29)**
- Run a bounded Black Friday or Cyber Monday promotion on the Pro tier only. Cap the discount window to avoid margin erosion.

**Week 19 (Nov 30 to Dec 6)**
- Target: 9 to 10 cumulative paying subscribers.
- Review cancellation save sequence performance, tighten if churn is appearing.

**Week 20 (Dec 7 to Dec 13)**
- Continue referral push. Personally follow up with warmest outreach leads.

**Week 21 (Dec 14 to Dec 20)**
- Target: 11 cumulative paying subscribers at Pro tier pricing, equivalent to $3,267 MRR.

**Week 22 (Dec 21 to Dec 27)**
- Hold. Avoid new offers during the holiday lull. Focus on retention of existing subscribers.

**Week 23 (Dec 28 to Dec 31)**
- Final checkpoint: confirm $3,000 or greater MRR is live and stable heading into January.

## Non Negotiables
- No paid traffic or public launch until every Phase 1 legal and operational item is closed.
- System operated, not service operated. If a task cannot be automated or delegated, it does not scale and does not belong in the weekly routine.
- Basic tier stays off the homepage. Protecting average revenue per subscriber matters more than maximizing signups.
- Inbound before outbound. Cold outreach does not activate until the lead magnet, nurture sequence, and testimonials are live.
- No new channels or tactics introduced after Week 18. The final six weeks are execution and retention only.

## Weekly Tracking Cadence
Every Friday, check three numbers against this plan: cumulative paying subscribers, current MRR, and week number versus the target in the Phase Overview table above. If a checkpoint is missed by more than one week, address it the following Monday rather than waiting for the next checkpoint.
