# Business Brief — Restaurant Skill Lab (AKR Operations)

Every agent in this project reads this file first. It is the shared context that keeps their work on-strategy. If it's ever out of date, update it here — every agent will pick up the change automatically next run.

## The business
Restaurant Skill Lab (consumer brand: Back of House Blueprint) is a subscription training and operations platform for independent restaurant owners, built by AKR Operations (legal entity: AKR Food and Beverage LLC).

## The goal
$3,000+ monthly recurring revenue by December 31, 2026. Target: 10-12 paying subscribers, weighted toward the Pro tier. Owner time is capped at under 2 hours per week — everything must be automatable or delegate-able. Nothing in the weekly routine should require hand-building.

## Pricing
- **Basic — $97/mo.** Downgrade path only. Never shown on the homepage or in marketing.
- **Pro — $297/mo.** The default, homepage offer. This is what all content and offers should point toward.
- **Enterprise — $597/mo.** Available for multi-location groups, not a primary focus.

## Audience
Independent and small-to-medium restaurant owners/operators (QSR, casual dining, bar, cafe) who are losing money to preventable back-of-house problems and don't have the time or budget for a consultant.

## Brand voice (Back of House Blueprint)
Speaks like a GM who has actually run a kitchen, not a consultant. Direct, practical, no fluff. Confident and encouraging, never condescending. Plain English over industry jargon. Systems language over theory.

Colors: Deep Teal `#123C39`, Charcoal Navy `#172227`, Amber `#D97D42`, Gold `#E8B354`, Cream `#F5F1E8`, Charcoal text `#2A2A26`.
Fonts: Outfit Bold (headlines), Big Shoulders Bold (display), Work Sans (body), DM Mono (labels/tags).

## The six content pillars
1. Labor and Staffing
2. Food Cost and Inventory
3. Customer Service and Recovery
4. Daily Operations and Systems
5. Management and Leadership
6. AI Business Operations — the core differentiator

## Non-negotiables (do not violate these in any agent output)
- No paid traffic or public launch until every Phase 1 legal/operational item is closed.
- Basic tier stays off the homepage and out of marketing content. Protecting average revenue per subscriber matters more than maximizing signups.
- Inbound before outbound. Cold outreach doesn't activate until the lead magnet, nurture sequence, and testimonials are live.
- No new channels or tactics after Week 18 (roughly mid-November 2026). The final stretch is execution and retention only.
- System operated, not service operated. If a task can't be automated or delegated, it doesn't belong in the weekly routine.

The complete 23-week, phase-by-phase roadmap is in `planning/full-plan.md`. Agents should treat this brief as the quick-reference summary and the full plan as the source of truth for dates and phase checkpoints.
